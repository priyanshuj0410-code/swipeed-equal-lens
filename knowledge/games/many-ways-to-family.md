**Node #g60: Chapter 7, ages 22+ (Building a Life).** *Real family is never only the biological kind, and a
setback is never a verdict.* The equity bookend of Chapter 7: where [If, When & Whether](if-when-whether.md) (#g59)
covers the biological-default decision, this node belongs to everyone whose path runs through adoption, fostering,
ART, surrogacy, single or LGBTQ+ parenthood, a blended family, or a childfree life. Lensy returns as a warm, honest
peer who doesn't sugar-coat the barriers. **The spine: every route is a real family, the routes are walked
honestly (steps, costs, emotions, and India's real legal barriers), and your worth never rests on which route you
take or whether it works the first time.**

## What it embodies

- **Every route is real family (14)**: busts *"real family is only biological"*; adoptive, single, queer, blended
  and childfree families are fully real.
- **The routes (14)**: what adoption, fostering, IVF/ART, surrogacy, and single & LGBTQ+ parenthood each involve,
  with the right official first step.
- **The real path (14)**: honest steps, timelines, costs and emotions; no romanticising, no false hope; **a
  setback isn't a verdict**.
- **Name the barriers (14)**: India's specific, gendered and legal barriers told honestly (CARA single-man
  girl-child rule; Surrogacy/ART altruistic-only & mainly-married; no same-sex marriage or joint adoption post-
  Supriyo) so people aren't blindsided.
- **Your family, your way (14)**: dignity, chosen family, support, and family acceptance on your terms.
- **Tools & help (14)**: the routes/eligibility reference, CARA and licensed clinics, current (dated) legal info.

**Stance.** Full dignity for every family form, none framed as lesser; LGBTQ+ content carries Spectrum's care (no
outing). The legal/eligibility content is **general, dated (2024-25) and evolving, not legal advice**, and is
explicitly flagged to be verified and localised (laws like Supriyo and CARA can change).
