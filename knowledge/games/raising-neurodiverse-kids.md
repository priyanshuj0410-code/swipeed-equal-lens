**Node #g67 - Chapter 8, Parent Layer (positive parenting).** *Difference, not deficiency - your child is
different, not less.* This is **[Same Same, Different](same-same-different.md) (#4) grown up**: the small child
learned that everyone is "same same, but different"; now the parent of an autistic, ADHD or learning-different
child learns that a brain that works differently is a difference to support, not a defect to fix. Lensy returns with
the same warmth. **The spine: lead with strengths; accommodate the environment instead of forcing the child to
mask; advocate for their rights; drop the shame (it's no one's fault); meet meltdowns with co-regulation; and look
after yourself, because this is demanding work.**

## What it embodies

- **Understand your child (14)** - the common neurodivergences, strengths-first, your child's own profile and
  pace; **different, not less**; brains naturally vary.
- **Accommodate (14)** - adjust the environment and expectations to the child rather than forcing them to mask;
  accommodations give fair access **like glasses**, they don't spoil.
- **Advocate (14)** - you are your child's key advocate; school accommodations, family understanding and services,
  with rights under the **RPwD Act 2016**; seek assessment without shame.
- **Drop the shame and blame (14)** - it's no one's fault - **not parenting, screens or vaccines**; meltdowns are
  overwhelm, not naughtiness; you don't grow out of it; stop hiding it.
- **Strengths and wellbeing (14)** - build on strengths and passions; calm co-regulation, not punishment; take
  co-occurring anxiety seriously; see the whole wonderful child, not a label.
- **Support and you (14)** - parent wellbeing matters and it is demanding; lean on peer parents and professionals;
  burnout → Looking After You (g63).

**Stance & safeguarding.** Strengths-based and **never deficit-shaming, never blaming parent or child**; accurate
and expert-reviewed; and **explicitly not a diagnostic tool** - it points to professionals for assessment. India:
neurodiversity is heavily stigmatised and under-recognised, delayed diagnoses are often blamed on parenting, and
school accommodations are a fight - so the node de-stigmatises, helps parents seek assessment and support without
shame, and points to rights under the RPwD Act 2016.
