**Node #36: THE FINALE lesson node (36 of 36)** (ages 15-18). *The feed is designed to use you, unless
you can read it.* The young person decodes the machine, the message and themselves, and steps into
adulthood as the wise, kind, critical person they've become. It is the **summit of the media thread**
([Smart Screen Heroes](smart-screen-heroes.md) #12, [Flip the Script](flip-the-script.md) #17,
[Reality Check](reality-check.md) #28) and a **synthesis of the critical thinking woven through every
game**. As the last lesson node, it is where **UN & RE make their final, *reflective* appearance**, not
to bust one more myth, but to look back on the hundreds the player has busted and **hand the skill over for
life**. **Lensy appears fully grown** here, the companion who began as a small shape-shifting friend in
[Feelings Friends](feelings-friends.md) (#1), a quiet marker that they grew up together.

- **Status:** live · https://swipeed.vercel.app/game/decoded

## How it works: five modes + the Badge Book
1. **Decode the Algorithm**: how feeds, recommendations and the attention economy work (you're shown what
   keeps you engaged, not just "what's out there"; filter bubbles; the business model is your time).
2. **Decode the Influence**: the **UN & RE** beat on manipulation ("if it's viral it's true", "I'm too
   smart to be manipulated", boss: "a dark pattern is just a design choice" → *name it and it loses its
   power*).
3. **Decode Yourself**: digital wellbeing: use it on purpose, protect your attention, privacy and digital
   footprint; intentional use beats the endless scroll.
4. **The Decoder**: the master **"decode anything"** tool: *Who made this & why? How did the algorithm
   serve it? What's the manipulation? What's actually true? How will I act?*
5. **Grow: Your Journey**: the **finale reflection**, with UN & RE's last words *"for years we erased the
   myths together"* → *"now you can do it yourself, for the rest of your life"* → **"Unlearn. Relearn.
   Grow."**, then the player writes a personal **digital-life charter** (use it on purpose · protect my
   attention & privacy · check before I share · be kind online · keep learning).

A 5-badge **Badge Book** finishes into the shared [`GameDone`](swipeed.md) card. Reuses the shared
**`UnReBeat`** + the voice model.
