# SwipeEd - Unlearn → Relearn → Grow

> **Owner: The Equal Lens.** *Unlearn. Relearn. Grow.* is [TEL](https://github.com/priyanshuj0410-code/owhile-engine/blob/c182048bd6c9f4f3c2ef73c6d08dfac8d5c8c1e2/knowledge/partners/the-equal-lens/index.md)'s
> organisational tagline and principle - TEL's IP, not Owhile's - as are the UN & RE characters and the
> erase-then-redraw interaction. SwipeEd, TEL's game, embodies it. This doc records how a customer's
> pedagogy maps onto engine mechanics, as proving-ground evidence.

**SwipeEd has one core learning principle, and everything else serves it: Unlearn → Relearn → Grow.**
Children are not empty cups we fill with facts. By the time they reach us they already hold ideas about
bodies, feelings, gender, safety and relationships - many half-true, picked up from peers, screens,
advertising and well-meaning adults. Real growth on these topics is as much about **letting go of an old
idea** as **taking on a new one**. UN erases; RE redraws; together they help a child grow - repeated
across the [spiral](swipeed.md) for fourteen years.

## Why this is the core principle
- **The spiral curriculum literally is unlearn-relearn.** Each age band revisits a topic deeper -
  returning means un-learning the simpler version and re-learning a truer one ("babies grow in a tummy"
  → "in the uterus, from an egg and sperm" → fertility → reproductive choices). The whole path is one
  long relearning ladder.
- **RSE/CSE is an *unlearning* subject.** Children arrive carrying myths and shame ("boys don't cry",
  "fair skin is better", "if he controls you he loves you", "she asked for it"). Teaching facts is half
  the job; dislodging what's already there is the other half.
- **It reframes being wrong as growth, not failure** - fitting SwipeEd's no-fail, non-shaming,
  growth-mindset ethos, and making changing your mind feel brave rather than embarrassing.
- **The science backs the pairing.** The *continued influence effect* shows that telling someone a myth
  is false rarely works unless it's **replaced** with a coherent better explanation. Erase alone fails;
  you must **erase AND redraw** - which is why the principle is always a duo, never UN alone.

## UN & RE - the Unlearn-Relearn duo
The principle is given a face by two characters a child roots for:

| | **UN** - the eraser | **RE** - the pencil |
|---|---|---|
| **Role** | Unlearns biases · erases old ideas · starts fresh | Relearns with empathy · draws new possibilities |
| **Personality** | Gentle, calm, never judgey; makes letting go feel safe | Curious, warm, hopeful; makes the new idea feel exciting |
| **Catchphrase** | "Let's gently rub that out…" | "…now let's draw what's really true." |

Shared catchphrase: **"Unlearn. Relearn. Grow."** UN never shames the child for the old idea; RE never
just states a fact - it draws a picture and gives a reason.

**Lensy hosts; UN & RE are the move.** Lensy is the constant, warm **in-game** companion the child
travels with for years (the *heart*). The path's travelling companion is a separate thing - the brand
flying ship (see [path world → the companion](swipeed-world.md#the-companion)). UN & RE are the
*method* - they appear at specific unlearn-relearn beats (a myth busted, a misconception corrected, a
topic revisited deeper), invited in by Lensy, then gone. They step in from ~age 6 (when children start
carrying myths home); for ages 3-6, Lensy handles gentle learning without a formal "you were wrong" beat.

## The ritual (the signature learning move)
When a genuine misconception surfaces, the game runs a short four-beat ritual:
1. **Surface** - the old idea comes up (a wrong swipe, a myth card, a "disguised" card). No buzzer.
2. **Unlearn (UN)** - gently rubbed out, without blame: "You're not wrong, you're growing."
3. **Relearn (RE)** - RE draws the truer picture *with a reason* (replace, don't just negate).
4. **Grow** - the relearn is celebrated and banked (a badge / crown level / a piece of the growing
   world); changing your mind is affirmed as the brave, grown-up thing.

Used with **restraint** - only at genuine misconception moments (myth-busts, corrections,
disguised-card reveals, spiral level-ups), not every lesson, and not for brand-new content. More
prominent through the teen years (media, porn myths, stereotypes, harmful norms). Honors School-Comfort
settings for the most culturally sensitive corrections.

## The design principles that follow
The principle is *named in* [SwipeEd's design principles](swipeed.md#design-principles); these are the
rules it implies:
- **You're not wrong, you're growing** - an old idea is a chance to grow, never a failure.
- **Changing your mind is brave** - the "Grow" beat celebrates belief-revision.
- **Erase gently, redraw with empathy** - UN is never harsh; RE explains, with care and a reason.
- **Replace, never just negate** - every unlearn is paired with a relearn that fills the gap (the
  science: debunking without a replacement doesn't stick).
- **Never blame the child - or their family** - myths are met where the child got them.
- **Everyone unlearns - even grown-ups** - parents/teachers are invited to unlearn too.
- **Relearning is the reward** - the points and pleasure sit on the "Grow" side (crowns, badges, world).

## How it powers games we already ship
The principle isn't a new feature - it names what the best games already do and gives them a shared soul:

| Game / mechanic | Unlearn (UN) | Relearn (RE) |
|---|---|---|
| [MythBuster: Gender](mythbuster-gender.md) (swipe) | "Boys are better leaders"; "girls are bad at maths" | Ability & leadership aren't about gender |
| [Flip the Script](flip-the-script.md) (media remix) | The stereotyped image the media sells | A fair, real portrayal you redraw |
| [Green Light / Red Light](green-light-red-light.md) (disguised cards) | "Constant texting means he loves me" | That's possessiveness - read the behaviour |
| Crown / spiral level-ups | The simpler, partial version of a topic | The deeper, truer version |
| The "look again" reveal (any swipe) | A wrong first read | The corrected read, and why |

UN & RE now runs **path-wide** - every age-band chapter uses the shared `UnReBeat`: e.g. Norm Storm's
"it's always been done this way", Boundary Bot's "no means no", Defenders of the Body's HIV myth-germs,
Mutual's consent myths, and Decoded's final, reflective beat that hands the skill over for life.

## Implementation status (built vs. designed)
- **Already embodied today:** myth-busting + reveals ([MythBuster](mythbuster-gender.md),
  [Flip the Script](flip-the-script.md)), disguised cards + "look again" in
  [Green Light / Red Light](green-light-red-light.md), and the [spiral path](swipeed-world.md) (each
  loop is a relearn).
- **UN & RE - now path-wide (debuted ages 6+):** the duo runs via the shared **`UnReBeat`** component
  (`components/games/un-re.tsx`) - first in [Body Lab Juniors](body-lab-juniors.md) (the colourism myth +
  the "where babies grow" relearn) and the ages-3-6 myth-pop precursors
  ([Same Same, Different](same-same-different.md), [Can-Do Kids](can-do-kids.md)) - and now in **every
  chapter** through to the final reflective beat in [Decoded](decoded.md).
- **Built since:** the constant in-game companion is named and present everywhere as **Lensy**, The
  Equal Lens mascot (`src/components/games/sam.tsx` still carries the old component name, but renders
  `/brand/lensy/lensy-*.svg`). **Still a future flourish:** a fuller, formal four-beat
  **ritual** UI (the current beat is UN-erase → RE-redraw).
