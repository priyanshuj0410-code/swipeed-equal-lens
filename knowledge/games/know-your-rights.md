**Node #g51: the Chapter 6 closer (College, ages 18-22).** You have rights at work, at home and online,
and they're only powerful if you know them. The Thread G adult node, building on
[Justice League: Rights](justice-league-rights.md) (#g35). On the [ModesEngine](swipeed-game-patterns.md).

## How it works: five modes
1. **Rights at Work**: contracts, pay, hours, leave; get it in writing (tap-reveal list).
2. **Harassment & POSH**: POSH Internal Committees; how to complain (scenes).
3. **Renting & Money Rights**: tenancy/deposits, consumer & cyber rights (scenes).
4. **Claim It**: the **UN → RE** beat busting "you can't fight the system" (redress routes, free legal aid).
5. **Tools & Ask-It**: rights/helpline card + private Q&A with **live-issue routing** (POSH ICC, NALSA 15100, 1930, 181/112).
