**Node #g48 - Chapter 6 (College, ages 18-22).** Money is freedom and confidence - budget it, dodge the
traps, know your worth, and keep money fair in love. On the shared [ModesEngine](swipeed-game-patterns.md).

## How it works - five modes
1. **Budget It** - split income into needs · wants · savings; pay yourself first (tap-reveal list).
2. **Save & Avoid Traps** - debt, EMIs, "Buy Now Pay Later" and OTP scams (scenes).
3. **Earn & Ask** - read a payslip; negotiate pay (closing the gap starts with asking).
4. **Money & Love** - the **UN → RE** beat: "he handles the money", and spotting financial control as abuse.
5. **Tools & Ask-It** - budget references + private Q&A with **abuse/scam routing** (Cyber Crime 1930, Women Helpline 181).
